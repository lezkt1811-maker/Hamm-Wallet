❓ FAQ

What are stickers made of?
Durable vinyl, die-cut, holographic, glitter, or matte finishes depending on the product — check each product page for its type.
How long does shipping take?
Orders are usually printed and packed within a few days, then shipped. You'll get a tracking number once it ships — check your order status any time by contacting us with your order number.
Can I request a custom sticker?
Yes! Use the Custom Order page — upload your image or describe your idea, and we'll show you a preview before you pay.
What do "Original / Parody-Inspired / Licensed / Public Domain" mean?
Original — designed from scratch by us.
Parody-inspired — our own take/doodle inspired by a broader trend, not a copy of a specific copyrighted image.
Licensed — we have explicit permission or a license to sell this design.
Public domain — the source artwork is not under copyright.
We label every product's design type internally so we stay on the right side of copyright and trademark law.
Can I return or exchange a sticker?
If your order arrives damaged or wrong, contact us within 14 days and we'll make it right.
Do you accept credit cards?
Yes, checkout is powered by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
