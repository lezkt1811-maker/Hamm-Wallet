About Us

💌 our_story.txt

We make pop-culture-inspired stickers with early-2000s energy — chrome, glitter, rainbow gradients, and that unapologetic MySpace-era chaos, rebuilt for right now.

Every sticker starts as an original design (or a clearly-labeled parody-inspired / licensed / public-domain piece — see our FAQ for what that means). We care about print quality: sharp lines, vivid color, and stickers that actually survive a water bottle.

This shop is small and independent — every order matters to us, and every design is made with actual care, not mass-produced clip art.
